We started PWA after watching leaders we love carry heavy administrative loads on top of their providential responsibilities — and deciding to do something practical about it.

Read Our Heart

Rooted in True Mother's Words

Our name comes from True Mother's teaching that pure water is clear, life-giving, and continually moving in order to stay pure. That is what we want to be for the providence: a clear, steady stream that refreshes leaders and keeps mission work moving.

Aligned with the Vision of Dr. Hak Ja Han, we serve so that pastors, Tribal Messiahs, and community leaders can give their full heart to Heavenly Parent's work — and still have meaningful time for their own families.
