A Word from Our Co-founder

Serving Leaders Is Personal for Us

Justin Okamoto
Co-founder · FFWPU Community Leader

Rooted in True Mother's Words

Our name comes from True Mother's teaching that pure water is clear, life-giving, and continually moving in order to stay pure. That is what we want to be for the providence: a clear, steady stream that refreshes leaders and keeps mission work moving.

Aligned with the Vision of Dr. Hak Ja Han, we serve so that pastors, Tribal Messiahs, and community leaders can give their full heart to Heavenly Parent's work — and still have meaningful time for their own families.

How We Serve

We Carry the Administration. You Carry the Mission.

Every ministry runs on a hundred small faithful tasks. Your dedicated assistant keeps them all moving.

Member & Guest Care

Membership records, follow-up with guests and new families, birthday and Blessing anniversary reminders.

Service & Event Support

Sunday service logistics, workshops, holy day celebrations — invitations, RSVPs, programs, and follow-up.

Community Communications

Weekly newsletters, announcements, social media, and inbox management — in your voice, on time.

Records & Reporting

Donation and tithing records, providential reporting, and clean books your board and HQ can trust.

Calendar & Visits

Pastoral visit scheduling, meeting coordination, and travel arrangements for tours and conferences.

Tribal Messiah Activity

Contact tracking, outreach follow-up, education scheduling, and materials for your Tribal Messiah work.
