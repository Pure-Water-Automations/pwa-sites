About Us

Built to Unburden Mission-driven Leaders

Pure Water Automations was founded in June 2025 by Justin Okamoto and Eunmi Rangala-Maddox, who saw leaders of meaningful organizations spending their best hours on paperwork instead of people — and built a team to change that.

Our name reflects our standard: pure water is clear, life-giving, and continually moving in order to stay pure. Rooted in a faith-driven commitment to service, we bring that clarity and steadiness to every organization we support.
