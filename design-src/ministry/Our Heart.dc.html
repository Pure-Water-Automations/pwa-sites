Our Heart

Serving the Providence, One Task at a Time

Pure Water Automations was founded in June 2025 by Justin Okamoto and Eunmi Rangala — two members who watched leaders they love carry heavy administrative loads on top of their providential responsibilities, and decided to do something practical about it.

Our name comes from True Mother's words: pure water is clear, life-giving, and continually moving in order to stay pure. We aim to be that stream for the leaders we serve — refreshing, faithful, and always moving.

Aligned with the Vision of Dr. Hak Ja Han

We believe every hour freed from paperwork is an hour returned to Heavenly Parent's work — to witnessing, to raising families, to caring for the people entrusted to each leader. That conviction shapes how we hire, how we train, and how we serve.
