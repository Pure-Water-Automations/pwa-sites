A Word About Grants

We prepare and submit. Funders decide.

Some agencies advertise a "success rate" for Google Ad Grant work. We won't do that, because it's easy to blur the difference between submitting a well-built application and getting approved — and only the funder controls the second part. Here's what we can tell you plainly instead:

No grant is guaranteed.

Approval decisions belong to the funder, and applications — especially the Google Ad Grant — often take multiple rounds of revisions to your website and materials.

It takes real hours.

A 20-hour/month plan is part-time support; grant readiness on that plan is a 3–6 month journey. Larger plans move faster.

The upside is real.

When a grant is awarded, our implementation work can often be written into the grant budget as a vendor line item — meaning the grant, not your general fund, pays for the work.

We'd rather set honest expectations now than surprise you later.

FAQ

Do we qualify for the nonprofit rate?

Registered 501(c)(3) organizations qualify. If your status has lapsed or you're not incorporated yet, ask us — helping organizations get (or restore) 501(c)(3) status is something we can quote separately.

Can you guarantee we'll get the Google Ad Grant?

No — and be cautious of anyone who says they can. We prepare and submit your application and handle the revision rounds, but Google makes the decision.

Who actually does the work?

A dedicated assistant from our trained VA team, supervised by a team leader, with specialists (web, design, media, bookkeeping) pulled in as needed.

What if it's not working?

Plans are month-to-month. Tell us within the first two weeks of a new engagement and we'll work with you to adjust it.

How do you handle sensitive information?

We work with organizations that handle donor and financial data, and we scope data access together during onboarding — you decide what stays in-house.
